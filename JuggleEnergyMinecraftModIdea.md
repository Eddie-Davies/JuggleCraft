Juggle Energy Minecraft Mod Idea
Overview
A Minecraft mod focused on realistic energy generation, storage, and distribution.The mod introduces a power network that connects generators, batteries, control blocks, andpowered devices.It is designed around the theme of industrial energy management, automation, andoptimisation.The player can build systems that decide where power goes, when batteries charge, and whichdevices receive priority.
Core Concept
The mod adds an energy system separate from vanilla redstone, but able to interact with it.Redstone devices can be powered through Juggle Energy if sufficient stored or generated energyis available.Each device requires a certain amount of power to activate or remain running.The system encourages players to manage supply, demand, storage, and efficiency.
Main Gameplay Loop
Generate power using one or more sources.Store excess power in batteries.Distribute power to machines, redstone devices, and automation blocks.Use a control system to prioritise important loads and avoid power shortages.Expand the network with better generators, larger batteries, and smarter control logic.
Power Generators
Coal Generator
Early-game and reliable power source.Burns coal or charcoal to produce energy.Produces steady output but requires fuel input.Suitable for basic setups and backup power.Could have upgrades for fuel efficiency or higher output.
Solar Power
Generates power during daylight.No fuel cost, but dependent on weather and time of day.Best used with battery storage to smooth out daytime-only production.Could be upgraded for better output, faster charging, or reduced weather penalties.
Wind Turbine
Generates power based on wind conditions and possibly height.Produces variable output depending on location and environment.• • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • •

Wave Power
Generates power near water or ocean environments.Themed as a renewable coastal energy source.Could depend on biome, water type, or weather activity.Useful for base locations near oceans or large bodies of water.
Battery Storage
Batteries store excess energy for later use.Different battery tiers provide increasing capacity and possibly better transfer rates.Batteries allow power networks to remain stable when generation drops.Stored power can be used at night, during storms, or when generators are offline.Higher-tier batteries could also be more efficient, faster to charge, or safer from overload.
Example Battery Tiers
Basic Battery: small storage, early-game use.Advanced Battery: medium storage, better transfer rate.High-Capacity Battery: large storage for bigger networks.Industrial Battery: very high storage for late-game automation systems.
Control Block: Jbox
The central control block of the system.Acts as the main interface for energy routing and automation rules.Lets players view generation, storage, demand, and active consumers.Can set priorities for devices and battery charging.Can enable or disable energy flow to specific systems.Can work as the main management block for an entire energy grid.
PLC Block Integration
PLC blocks can connect to the Jbox for smarter control logic.PLCs can trigger events based on energy conditions.Example triggers:Turn on a generator when battery charge drops below a set level.Shut off non-essential machines during low-power conditions.Reserve power for critical systems.Switch between generators automatically.This adds an industrial automation feel and gives players more advanced control.
Redstone Device Interaction
Redstone devices can be powered or activated through the energy system.Each device can have a defined energy cost.Devices only run when enough power is available.Devices may stop, slow down, or fail to activate if the network cannot meet demand.• • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • •

Dynamic Redstone Network Detection
The system should automatically detect linked redstone circuits connected to the energynetwork.Power usage is calculated based on the total size and complexity of the redstone system.The more components in a circuit (repeaters, comparators, pistons, dispensers, etc.), the higherthe energy requirement.Redstone wire length contributes to power cost, simulating transmission overhead.Large or complex automation builds require significantly more sustained power.Encourages efficient circuit design rather than excessive or redundant wiring.
Example Scaling Factors
Short, simple circuits: low power consumption.Medium circuits with multiple components: moderate power consumption.Large automated systems with extensive wiring: high power consumption.Highly complex contraptions (e.g. farms, sorting systems, logic systems): very high powerconsumption.
Example Device Costs
Doors, pistons, lamps, and simple mechanisms: low power cost.Dispensers, repeaters, rails, and moving systems: moderate power cost.Complex automated systems: high power cost.Large industrial machines: very high power cost.
Energy Management Features
Priority system for deciding which devices stay online first.Load balancing across batteries and generators.Automatic switching between power sources.Warning states for low power or overloaded circuits.Optional manual override for player control.Energy dashboards showing production, consumption, and storage.
Progression and Balance
Start with basic coal power and small batteries.Move into renewable sources and medium-tier storage.Unlock advanced control systems and high-capacity infrastructure.Late-game gameplay focuses on optimisation, automation, and efficient grid design.Balance should reward smart planning rather than simply placing more generators.
Possible Tiered Progression
Early game: coal generator, basic battery, simple Jbox control.Mid game: solar and wind power, advanced batteries, basic PLC logic.Late game: wave power, industrial batteries, complex automation, full power routing control.• • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • •

Visual and Thematic Direction
Industrial, technical, and clean design language.Blocks should look functional and modular.Clear status indicators for power levels, charging, and active output.GUI should show simple but detailed energy data.Strong Juggle Energy branding across blocks and interfaces.
Player Benefits
Adds a deeper automation system to Minecraft.Gives more purpose to power generation and storage.Lets players build realistic microgrid-style systems.Creates new technical challenges and engineering-style gameplay.Works well for players who enjoy automation, factory building, and optimisation.
Remote Connectivity System (4G & SIM Integration)
Overview
Introduces remote control of energy systems via a simulated 4G network.Players can interact with their energy grid (via Jbox) from anywhere within network coverage.Adds a layer of infrastructure building focused on communication, not just power.
Core Components
SIM Card
Crafted item required to enable network connectivity.Acts as an authentication and connection medium between player devices and Jbox systems.
Online Jbox
Created by combining a standard Jbox with a SIM card.Gains remote connectivity capabilities.Can receive commands and send status data over the 4G network.
Player Device (Smart Controller)
Handheld item used to interface with Online Jboxes.Allows players to:Trigger redstone events (e.g. pistons, switches).Enable or disable systems remotely.Monitor power usage, battery levels, and active devices.
4G Tower System
4G Tower (Base Station)
Provides signal coverage in a defined radius.Both the player and the Online Jbox must be within signal coverage to communicate.Towers require a continuous power source to operate.• • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • • 
4
Can be powered by batteries, solar panels, wind turbines, or other generators.If power is lost, the tower goes offline and network connectivity is interrupted.Encourages integration with Jbox systems to manage uptime and prioritise tower power.
Signal Coverage Mechanics
Each tower has a circular coverage radius.Signal strength could degrade at the edge of the radius (optional feature).No connection is possible outside of coverage areas.
Network Expansion
Multiple 4G towers can be placed to extend coverage.Towers can form chains or networks to cover large areas of the map.Encourages players to build infrastructure across their world.
Gameplay Mechanics
Remote actions consume a small amount of energy from the network.Loss of power to towers disables communication.Encourages redundancy and backup systems for critical remote operations.Can integrate with PLC logic for advanced automation (e.g. remote overrides, alerts).
Example Use Cases
Remotely activate a piston door from anywhere in range.Shut down non-essential systems during power shortages.Monitor base energy usage while exploring.Trigger automated farms or defenses remotely.
Expansion Ideas
Power cables and transfer lines with loss over distance.Transformers for stepping power up or down.Fuses and breakers to protect circuits.Wireless monitoring or remote control through advanced blocks.Energy alarms and emergency shutoff systems.Integration with machines, farms, security systems, and transportation.
Summary
Juggle Energy is a Minecraft mod built around energy generation, storage, and automatedcontrol.It adds realistic power management to redstone-based gameplay.The Jbox acts as the main control hub, with PLC blocks providing logic and automation.Multiple generator types and battery tiers create a progression system.The end result is a flexible industrial energy network that rewards planning and optimisation.
